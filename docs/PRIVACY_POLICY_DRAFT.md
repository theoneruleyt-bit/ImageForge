# ImageForge Privacy Policy — Draft for publisher review

**Important:** Replace the publisher/contact placeholders and publish this policy at a stable public URL before Play production submission. Verify it against the exact final app and Play Console Data safety answers.

ImageForge is designed to perform its core image-processing features on the user's device. Users select images for actions such as compression, resizing, format conversion, batch processing, metadata inspection/removal, preview, export and sharing.

ImageForge does not require an ImageForge account for its core image-processing workflow. ImageForge Pro purchases are handled by Google Play. Google Play may process purchase and account-related information under Google's own terms and privacy practices; the app receives purchase state needed to provide the purchased entitlement.

ImageForge may read supported metadata from images selected by the user when the user uses metadata/privacy features. When the user chooses metadata removal, ImageForge creates a processed output according to that action. Local history/settings used by the app remain on the device unless the user or operating system backs up, transfers, shares, or removes that data.

The app uses Android system mechanisms for selecting, saving and sharing user-requested files. ImageForge does not claim that removing supported metadata removes every possible identifying characteristic from an image.
